# City Curator: five-person phone check

Ask five people who have not seen the site to use it on their own phones. Do not guide them while they work. Note the device, whether browser cookies and local storage are enabled, time taken, and the first place they hesitate.

1. Find a free Istanbul route that fits within two hours. What did the filters mean to you?
2. Open its details. Identify the starting point, approximate time, and next stop.
3. Start the route and open a stop in a map app. Was the difference between the in-site preview and walking directions clear?
4. Sign in and save the route, refresh, then find it in Saved. Note every failure or unexpected screen.
5. Share the route link with yourself and describe what information is missing before going out.

For each task record: success without help (yes/no), time, unexpected taps, exact quotes, and whether the participant would use the route. Fix repeated blockers first, then rerun the affected task.
